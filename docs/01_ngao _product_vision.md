# Ngao Product Vision

**Project:** Ngao
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** MVP Baseline
**Tagline:** Protect what sober-you built.

---

## 1. Overview

Ngao is a mobile financial self protection and recovery support application.

It is designed for people who recognize a recurring relationship between difficult or high risk moments and impulsive financial decisions.

The product is based on one central principle:

> **Don't ask future-you to make a decision that sober-you could make today.**

Ngao helps users make important financial decisions while clear headed and then introduces friction, reminders, support, and reflection when they enter a predictable high risk moment.

---

## 2. Origin

Ngao was inspired by an observation within the creator's own community.

A close relative was struggling with alcohol use and poor financial discipline. The important observation was not simply that money was being spent irresponsibly.

The deeper pattern was that sensible decisions were often made while sober, but those decisions could become difficult to maintain during periods of stress, cravings, emotional pressure, drinking, or other high risk situations.

The resulting cycle could look like:

**Stress → craving → alcohol becomes accessible → money becomes accessible → impulsive spending → essential funds are depleted → financial stress → regret → recovery attempt → another trigger**

This led to a different framing of the problem.

The problem is not simply:

> "How do we teach someone to budget?"

The more important question is:

> **"How do we help someone protect the financial decisions they made while clear headed when they enter a high risk state?"**

That question became Ngao.

---

## 3. Problem

Traditional budgeting applications generally assume that the user's ability to make financial decisions is relatively consistent.

That assumption can fail when decision making changes with context or state.

A budgeting application may tell someone:

> "You have KSh 50,000 remaining this month."

That information may be accurate without being sufficient.

The user may already know what the money should be used for. The problem occurs when a high risk moment makes an impulsive decision more likely.

Ngao therefore focuses on protecting **previously chosen intentions** rather than simply presenting financial information.

---

## 4. Product Insight

Ngao combines four ideas:

**Financial protection**

Help users define what their money is intended for.

**Behavioral interruption**

Introduce time and friction between an urge and an impulsive decision.

**Social support**

Make it easier to contact a trusted person during a difficult moment.

**Pattern recognition**

Use previous events to help identify recurring high risk periods.

The product becomes:

**financial protection + behavioral interruption + social support + pattern recognition**

---

## 5. Core Product Loop

The product is structured around five stages.

### PLAN

While sober, the user defines what their money is for.

Examples:

* rent
* family
* savings
* emergency fund
* debt
* food
* education
* flexible spending

### PREDICT

The user defines situations and periods that tend to be difficult.

Examples:

* payday
* Friday evenings
* weekends
* stress
* loneliness
* arguments
* social events
* alcohol availability
* unexpected money

The MVP uses deterministic rules rather than machine learning.

### INTERRUPT

When the user experiences an urge, Ngao changes the context.

Instead of showing a normal budgeting dashboard, it presents a focused intervention.

The user can:

* start a cooldown
* play a sober message
* contact a trusted person
* review protected priorities
* indicate that they have changed their environment

### PROTECT

Ngao introduces friction when a spending decision conflicts with the user's previously defined protection plan.

The MVP cannot physically block a banking transaction.

It can:

* warn
* require deliberate confirmation
* encourage a cooldown
* show protected commitments
* record an override

### LEARN

After an event, the user can reflect on:

* what triggered the moment
* whether money was spent
* what helped
* what should change next time

Ngao uses this history to produce simple pattern insights.

---

## 6. Target User

The initial target user is an adult who recognizes that certain situations can make impulsive financial decisions more likely.

The initial and most urgent use case concerns financial harm associated with problematic alcohol use.

However, the underlying product concept is broader.

The long term product may support other recurring high risk states where users want to protect decisions made during calmer periods.

---

## 7. Product Personality

Ngao should feel:

* calm
* protective
* private
* respectful
* trustworthy
* empowering
* modern

Ngao should not feel:

* clinical
* judgmental
* punitive
* childish
* gamified
* like a traditional banking dashboard

---

## 8. Language Principles

Use language that preserves the user's dignity and agency.

Prefer:

* difficult moment
* high risk period
* urge
* protection
* support
* commitment
* reflection
* reset

Avoid:

* failure
* weak
* irresponsible
* addict
* alcoholic
* relapse failure
* you broke your streak
* you failed

Ngao is not a medical or clinical product.

---

## 9. MVP Vision

The first version should prove that a user can:

1. Define what they want to protect.
2. Define when they are most vulnerable.
3. Activate support during an urge.
4. Experience financial friction.
5. Reflect afterward.
6. See simple patterns over time.

The goal is not to prove that Ngao can cure alcohol dependence.

The goal is to prove the **protective decision making workflow**.

---

## 10. Long Term Vision

The initial MVP is a local mobile implementation.

The long term product may eventually support:

* real financial integrations
* M-Pesa integration
* bank integrations
* actual protected funds
* transaction level controls
* stronger personalization
* advanced pattern prediction
* push notifications
* multi device synchronization
* regional expansion
* multiple currencies
* professional recovery resources

These are future capabilities and are not part of the current MVP unless explicitly added through a new product decision.

---

## 11. Success Hypotheses

The first product hypotheses are:

### Hypothesis 1

Users can define meaningful financial commitments while clear headed.

### Hypothesis 2

A focused intervention can create useful friction during an urge.

### Hypothesis 3

Trusted person support can be meaningfully integrated into the high risk experience.

### Hypothesis 4

Recurring events can reveal patterns that help users improve future protection plans.

---

## 12. Product Metrics

Potential future product metrics include:

**Financial protection rate**

Percentage of planned essential income remaining protected after a high risk period.

**Intervention rate**

Percentage of urge events in which the intervention flow was activated.

**Cooldown completion rate**

Percentage of intervention events where the cooldown was completed.

**Support engagement**

Percentage of events where the trusted person was contacted.

**Protected payday rate**

Percentage of salary cycles where planned essential allocations remain intact.

**Repeat high risk frequency**

Change in reported high risk events over time.

These metrics should be used to evaluate product effectiveness rather than relying only on generic streak counts.

---

## 13. Guiding Principle

Ngao does not attempt to control the user.

It helps the user protect decisions they already made for themselves.

> **Sober-you already knows what matters. Ngao helps protect it.**
